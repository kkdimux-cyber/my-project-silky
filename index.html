Why is this so confusing?

SystemHow it worksWhere you meet it
International (man nai)0 at birth, +1 on each birthdayOfficial standard for legal and administrative matters
Year age (yeon nai)This year − birth yearSome laws: alcohol, tobacco, military service
Traditional (seneun nai)1 at birth, +1 every January 1Older custom, still heard in casual talk
Didn't Korea abolish “Korean age”?

On June 28, 2023, a law confirmed international age as the default for legal and administrative matters. It did not ban people from using other counts in daily life, and some laws still use year age.

When can I buy alcohol or cigarettes?

Under the Youth Protection Act, from January 1 of the year you turn 19. Your birthday does not matter. Shops may ask for ID.

What about school and the military?

Children normally start elementary school in March of the year after they turn 6. Military service rules (which mainly apply to Korean men) are also organized by birth year.

Why do Koreans ask how old I am so quickly?

Age affects speech level and what you call each other, such as eonni, oppa, nuna and hyeong. People often ask “What year were you born?” (Myeot nyeonsaeng-ieyo?). Answering with your birth year avoids confusion.
