What Order to Apply Skincare Products

You do not need many products, and there is no required number of steps. A short routine of cleansing, moisturizer and daytime sunscreen is a reasonable start.

A common order

Morning: cleanser, then any toner, essence or serum, then moisturizer, then sunscreen.

Evening: cleansing oil or balm if you use one, water-based cleanser, any treatment layers, then moisturizer.

Layers usually go from thinnest to thickest. If a product label or a doctor gives a different instruction, follow that.

Double cleansing

Double cleansing means using an oil or balm first to loosen sunscreen and makeup, then a water-based cleanser. Not everyone needs it. If your skin feels tight or irritated, use a gentler routine.

Sunscreen

The American Academy of Dermatology advises a broad-spectrum, water-resistant sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher. Apply it about 15 minutes before going outdoors. Reapply about every two hours outdoors, and after swimming or sweating. See its sunscreen questions and answers. Shade and protective clothing help too.

Adding new products

Add one product at a time and patch-test it, so you can tell what caused a reaction. Start exfoliants and retinoids slowly, and do not use them together on the same night without advice. If you are pregnant, have a skin condition or use prescription products, ask a doctor.

This is general information, not medical advice. Build your routine.
