Korean Shoe and Clothing Sizes

Shoes are in millimetres

Korean shoe sizes use millimetres, based on foot length. A size of 250 means a size for a foot of about 250 mm, or 25 cm.

How to measure your foot

  1. Put a sheet of paper on a hard floor against a wall.
  2. Stand on it with your heel touching the wall.
  3. Mark the end of your longest toe, holding the pen upright.
  4. Measure from the wall edge of the paper to the mark.
  5. Repeat for the other foot and use the longer one.

Shoe shape and width also affect fit. Some shoes come only in 5 mm or 10 mm steps.

Women's clothing numbers

You may see 44, 55, 66, 77 and 88 on Korean women's clothing. These are labels, not a measurement that you can read directly in centimetres. Brands do not all cut them the same way.

Men's tops

Numbers such as 95, 100 and 105 relate to chest size in centimetres. Brands use them differently, so compare the measurements of the garment.

“Free size”

“Free size” means the item comes in one size. It does not mean it fits everyone.

Before you buy

Check the brand's size chart and the return rules. Any general conversion, including ours, is only a starting point.
