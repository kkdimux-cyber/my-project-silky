What you type into the tools

Names, birthdays and other choices are processed in your browser. This site does not send them to a server or save them.

What is stored on your device

If you press the light or dark mode button, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the page can remember it. You can remove it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To deliver and protect the site, Cloudflare processes request data such as visitors' IP addresses. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
